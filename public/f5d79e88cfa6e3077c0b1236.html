Mason Lead Gen: What We Found and What We Changed

Mason's own paid media, tracking and landing pages, Oct 1 – Nov 3, 2026, with changes made Nov 4, 2026. Internal.

We looked into why traffic looks big but real leads are rare. The short version: since the new site launched on Sept 21, none of our ad platforms could see a lead. With no lead signal, every platform optimized for cheap clicks, and that is what we bought. About 86% of our October site traffic was paid, and none of the 4 real prospects came from paid media.

~$10.7Kpaid spend, Oct 1 – Nov 3 (Meta, Google Ads, YouTube)
~42K → 34Kpaid clicks and visits bought → GA4 sessions recorded
30 → 4form fills since launch → real prospects (3 Organic, 1 Tradeshow)
0lead conversions recorded by Google Ads, GA4 or Meta since launch

What we did on Nov 4: fixed lead tracking on every platform, paused the junk spend, cut Audience Network on Meta, and fixed 9 broken sitelinks. Next: point search ads at the /go/ landing pages.

Meta bought accidental taps. Audience Network (in-app banners and rewarded video) took $1,329 for 19,763 clicks at $0.07 each. On Nov 3 one ad hit a 35% click-through rate, which isn't people choosing to click. Spend never changed; on Oct 8 Meta moved delivery to $5–8 CPM inventory by itself, because it had no lead signal to steer by.

Google's Demand Gen behaved the same way. $2,721 for 1,533 clicks, 0 conversions, and a 0.0–0.2% conversion rate in every quarter since Q2 2025.

The YouTube promo bought foreign traffic. 14,736 "website visits" for $66, including 676 sessions from Turkey.

Current prospects: 3ZRO ($3K, Solutions, meeting Nov 5), Dazbog ($3.5K, Analysis), Luxorro (Lost), College of the Atlantic (going another direction; should be marked Lost).

Follow-up gap: in Q4 2025, only 3 of 21 prospects show any reply from Mason.

Root Causes

  • Google Ads counted a page nobody reaches. The only lead conversion fired on learn.masoninteractive.com/thank-you. The new site's forms show an inline thank-you message and never go there, so there were 0 conversions from Sept 21 on. YouTube subscriptions and views were also Primary goals, so Google optimized toward them.
  • GA4 had no lead event: 0 key events.
  • Meta's Lead event wasn't added until Oct 29. The campaign was "Learning limited" from launch day, so Meta drifted into Audience Network and Reels overlays.
  • No ad points at a landing page with a form. 173 of 176 Google search clicks went to the homepage; Meta goes to /services/ and the homepage. The /go/ pages are built and tracked but get no traffic.
  • Broken links. 9 sitelinks went to 404s or the old Unbounce site, and any link to masoninteractive.com/<page> without the www still 404s.
