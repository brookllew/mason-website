The Colleges of Law: Ads Driving to the Juris Doctor Page

Prepared Nov. 5, 2026 · Google Ads data Oct. 6 – Nov. 4, 2026 · Landing page: collegesoflaw.edu/learn-juris-doctor

Before reading: Performance Max is reported at the asset-group level. Google's asset-group export does not include final URLs, and Performance Max can send people to other pages on the site. The two asset groups below are in the Juris Doctorate Performance Max campaign; their numbers cover all of their traffic, not only visits to this page.

Over the last 30 days, 24 live ads and asset groups sent paid traffic to the Juris Doctor page across three Google campaign types. Together they spent $18,446 and drove 69.33 conversions. Search does most of the work: 18 responsive search ads account for 87% of spend and 61.33 of the conversions, led by the General Legal Terms and Juris Doctorate campaigns.

The rest is upper-funnel. Four YouTube videos in the Brand Demand Gen campaign point here, and two Performance Max asset groups run images, video and copy across Google's network. Every ad is shown below as it appears, with its numbers for the period.

Recommendations

Based on Oct. 6 – Nov. 4 performance. Draft copy is for review; claims should be confirmed with The Colleges of Law before launch.

Fix now

Remove the "ABA Accredited Law School" headline from the top-performing ad

The best ad in the account (Law School California, General Legal Terms) includes the headline "ABA Accredited Law School." The Colleges of Law's J.D. is accredited by the State Bar of California's Committee of Bar Examiners and by WSCUC, not by the ABA; the school's own site describes it as a non-ABA J.D. Google can show this headline in any rotation. Replace it with an accurate accreditation line such as "State Bar-Accredited J.D." (confirm wording with COL). It is the only live ad with the claim.

Scale what works

Use the Law School California ad as the template for new variants

This one ad drove 14.5 conversions at $137 each, 24% of all search conversions to this page, against a search average of $261. It is rated Excellent and leads with California, bar-pass and access messages. We suggest adding a second version of it in each search ad group, swapping the shared "community-oriented" headlines for proof points the winning ad and the stronger J.D. ad already use. Draft headlines (character count shown, 30 max):

  • State Bar-Accredited J.D. 25
  • No LSAT Required 16
  • Part-Time J.D. in California 28
  • Evening J.D. Classes in CA 26
  • Law School for Working Adults 29
  • Earn Your J.D. While Working 28

Draft descriptions (90 max):

  • Earn your J.D. part-time while you work. No LSAT required. Request info today. 78
  • Our J.D. is accredited by the State Bar of California Committee of Bar Examiners. 81
  • No LSAT needed. Evening classes and bar prep built in. See if you qualify today. 80
Rewrite

Replace the weaker ad in J.D. Degree, the biggest-spending ad group

J.D. Degree spent $2,979. Its two ads perform very differently: one cost $1,439 per conversion (1.33 conv. on $1,914), the other $266. The weak ad relies on the same generic "community-oriented" and "diversity" headlines used across the account; the strong one is specific (California J.D., bar exam prep, Ventura and Santa Barbara). Pause or rewrite the weak ad using the drafts above. Then check this ad group's search terms: if online, hybrid and campus searches are mixed together, split it into separate ad groups so each gets matching copy.

Restructure

Give "Become A Lawyer" searchers their own message, or their own page

Become A Lawyer spent $716 for 1 conversion, with click-through rates near the bottom of the search ads here (3.1% and 3.2%). One of its ads is rated Poor: its headlines are campus names ("Ventura Law School", "Santa Barbara Law School") rather than an answer to "how do I become a lawyer." These are early-stage searchers landing on a program page. Two options: new ads with matching headlines, or a dedicated "How to become a lawyer in California" landing page (no LSAT, part-time path, bar prep). If neither is worth it, shift this budget to Law School California.

  • How to Become a Lawyer in CA 28
  • Start Law School Without LSAT 29
  • Your Path to the CA Bar 23
  • Become a Lawyer in California 29
Landing pages

Test campus and brand landing pages, and pull Quality Scores

Every live ad here, including brand and campus ad groups, sends traffic to the same page. Santa Barbara Campus and Ventura Campus searchers might convert better on a campus page (older Ventura ads used /welcome-ventura/), and brand searchers often expect the home page. The exports used here don't include keyword Quality Score, so we can't yet say whether this page's landing page experience is below average; the next step is a keyword report with Quality Score components for these campaigns. If landing page experience is low, a new or rebuilt page lifts every ad group at once.

Tune

Smaller fixes

  • Brand: the SB+Ventura ad has only 9 headlines and no conversions on $517; add headlines to reach 15. In Core Brand, one ad converts at $157 and the other at $543.
  • Ad strength: 12 of 18 search ads carry Google's suggestion to work more keywords into headlines and descriptions.
  • Demand Gen: the Carlos Salas Spotlight :30 takes 74% of video spend at $0.53 per view, while Faculty Brand :30 runs at $0.24. Worth testing a more even split. Two ads in the "Juris Doctorate (J.D.)" ad group point to the Hybrid J.D. page and belong in the Hybrid J.D. ad group.
  • Performance Max, Hybrid J.D.: 236,942 impressions but 3 conversions, Average strength, and no uploaded images (only Google's landing-page previews). Add the image set used by Santa Barbara & Ventura, and check whether this group should send people to the Hybrid J.D. page instead.
