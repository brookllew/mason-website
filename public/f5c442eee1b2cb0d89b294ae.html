What we heard

  • Your remit is direct to learner. A separate SNHU team sells to employers and partners.
  • Generative AI and Data Literacy are the early leaders.
  • Stackability is a real differentiator: finish a course and you are part of the way to a degree.
  • skills.snhu.edu sits on its own subdomain, apart from the main site and its marketing.
  • A product-by-product campaign about 18 months ago didn't build momentum.

What's changing

  • Today the site is checkout only. Lead capture and HubSpot email nurture go live in the next couple of months.
  • By the end of January the site can run bundles, buy-one-get-one, referral and friends-and-family pricing.
  • From January, courses can be bought in two-week modules, with short-form content planned as a feeder.
  • There is no admissions team. Nurture runs by email.

What success looks like

  • More than 10 new paid enrollments.
  • An acquisition cost per enrollment below the $2,999 price.
  • Timed to the March, May and June starts, before the fiscal year ends June 30.
  • Credibility, not just traffic: learner stories, cohort results, badges shared on LinkedIn.

How to read this

The situation

SNHU's Professional Skills courses are 6-week, non-credit, $2,999, bought by credit card on skills.snhu.edu. Marcy owns the direct-to-learner side; a separate SNHU team sells to employers. There has been almost no marketing: about 10 paid learners to date, mostly alumni. There is no admissions team, and lead capture plus HubSpot email nurture go live in the next couple of months.

Her definition of success for a first test is more than 10 paid enrollments at an acquisition cost below the sticker price, aimed at the March, May and June starts. Her fiscal year ends June 30.

How the numbers work

Every tier runs the same chain, one month at a time:

  1. Media buys leads. Performance media divided by cost per lead. We start at $88, the average across 21 certificate and credential landing pages in Mason's own Google Ads accounts over the last 12 months.
  2. Some leads are junk. At The Community Solution, 73% of leads reach the CRM; the rest are duplicates or junk.
  3. Some valid leads buy. TCS enrolls about 4% of CRM leads, but that includes multi-year doctoral programs with an admissions process. SNHU's course is short, cheaper and sold with a buy-now checkout, so the base case here is 6%, with 4% and 10% as the low and high cases.
  4. Cost per enrollment is shown two ways: media only, and all-in with Mason's monthly fees. Setup and the commercial are one-time and sit outside the monthly math.

How the tiers differ

Tier A is the smallest program that can prove the funnel: systems setup, search and social, performance creative.

Tier B adds the three things that lower cost per enrollment: landing page optimization with Accipiter, AI search visibility, and creators paid only on sales. It is the tier we recommend.

Tier C adds a commercial, connected TV and out-of-home to build the Professional Skills name. Buying the commercial lowers the management fee.
