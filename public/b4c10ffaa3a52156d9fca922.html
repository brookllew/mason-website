Mason 2027 Sales Plan

Working draft, Nov 5, 2026, for review with the sales team. Every number here can change. Internal.

The goal is $1M in new business in 2027, counted as money clients actually pay us. That takes 21 deals: 15 retainers and 6 production projects. It does not take more deals than we already close. It takes bigger deals, and clients who stay.

What has to change: production has to more than double, from $123K sold in 2026 to $300K, and existing clients carry the biggest share of it. Client budget growth and overall churn stay with operations and are not in this plan.

The Rules

How we count, so everyone reads the same number.

RuleWhat it means
One goal, two linesRecurring and one-time production both count toward the $1M, tracked separately.
Real money onlyA client who leaves counts only for what they paid.
Early losses are assumedWe plan on a third of new retainers leaving at day 90, whatever the contract says. If the newer contracts hold, we beat the plan.
Cross-sells countA new service sold to a current client counts. proposed A client's ad budget growing stays with operations.
January deals countDeals are valued over 12 months from signing. A July deal pays only half of that inside calendar 2027, so early deals matter most.

What's in a full client: $2K strategy + the greater of $2K or 3% of ad spend for media + $2K creative. The 3% takes over at about $67K a month in spend, so a $100K-a-month client is $7K a month. Bigger spenders mean bigger deals with no extra selling.

Creative on its own gets its own line: $2K a month inside a full client, and $5K or $8.5K a month as a standalone retainer on the March rate card, which may be out of date. A standalone creative client would be a separate deal.

Affiliate is sold as a $5K setup plus commission. Setups count as one-time money. The commission rate isn't set, so commission is left out of the $1M.

Accipiter's 1% isn't counted, because we haven't settled what it's charged on.
