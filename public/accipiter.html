Accipiter — by Mason

Every platform. Every campaign. One place to run them — with a person in charge.

Accipiter is the operating layer Mason built to run modern advertising. It watches every account all night, finds what broke, explains why, and drafts the fix — then puts it in front of a human before anything goes live.

Meta Google TikTok The Trade Desk Klaviyo Salesforce GA4 Creative + more

The 7 a.m. problem

Advertising was supposed to help. Now it's as complicated as the problem it solves.

You run marketing for a mission-driven institution. Enrollment targets went up this year. The budget didn't. Every stakeholder has a priority, and they don't all agree. That part is normal. That's the job.

But you probably have four major ad platforms — plus email, a CRM, analytics, and creative — with dozens of campaigns across them, all producing data around the clock, and none of them telling you which part matters. So at seven in the morning, someone on your team opens a stack of dashboards and starts looking for the thing that went wrong overnight. Because somewhere in there, something did.

The solution

Accipiter is the operating layer we built to run it all — in one place.

It's connected to the four platforms where your budget actually lives — Meta, Google, TikTok, and The Trade Desk — plus the systems around them: Klaviyo, Salesforce, GA4, your creative, and more. It has been watching every account and every campaign all night. Back to that Tuesday morning — here's what it does with it.

01

It finds the problem

A campaign that's still spending, but stopped converting three days ago. Accipiter surfaces it before your team opens the first dashboard.

02

It explains why

Not just a flag. Maybe the creative has worn out; maybe the same people have seen the same ad too many times. It tells you the reason.

03

It drafts the fix

Pause that ad set, move the budget to one that's still working, and cue a fresh creative — written up and ready to review.

04

It puts it in front of a person

The most important part. Nothing goes live until someone on your team says yes.

Built by operators, not a software company

The advantage

Your campaign data tells you what happened in your account. Accipiter knows the pattern.

It knows what has happened across these platforms over eighteen years — which interventions generally work, which generally don't, and what the numbers tend to look like right before something needs to change.

No client's results are ever visible to another, and it runs on ad-platform data, not student records. So when Accipiter recommends a move, it isn't guessing from one account — it's recognizing a pattern Mason has seen before.

A person in charge

Accipiter uses AI to read and sort faster than any team could. What it does not do is decide on its own.

Human approval on every action

Every recommendation goes to a person before it goes live. The operator reads it, checks the reasoning, and says yes or no.

Hard limits

Every automated action has a limit it can't cross without a human approving it. No surprises, no runaway spend.

A kill switch

One control stops everything instantly, across every account and platform.

It doesn't replace the operator

It lets someone who knows advertising review more, notice more, respond faster, and hold on to what they've learned.
