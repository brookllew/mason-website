Pacific Oaks College Google Ads and Meta: What Changed Since Q4 2025

Google Ads and Meta performance for Pacific Oaks College, January 2024 to September 2026, plus early results for Oct. 1–12, 2026. Prepared by Mason Interactive for The Community Solution Education System.

Pacific Oaks College is spending more for about the same number of inquiries. Google Ads produced about 790 inquiries a month in Q4 2025 and about 745 in July to September 2026, while monthly Google Ads spend rose 47%, from about $112K to about $166K, and cost per inquiry went from $142 to $223. With Meta included, the two channels produced about 1,075 inquiries a month then and about 1,055 now, at $136 and $188 per platform inquiry. Q4 2025 also looks better than it was: that quarter Performance Max recorded far more inquiry forms per click than in any other period, and many were not real inquiries. Against the same months last year, search inquiries are down about 19% on 9% more search spend. Two things drove it. First, budget was directed to the graduate programs, where each inquiry admissions contacts costs $1,000 or more: The Community Solution kept the M.S.W. and M.F.T. budgets in place in May and confirmed M.S.W. as the top program in August. Meanwhile Early Childhood Education (ECE) and teacher credentials, the lowest-cost degree programs, received less and reached fewer of the searches that had worked for them. Second, search clicks cost 32% more. Meta inquiries cost less on the platform but convert below Google's, so Meta adds volume rather than replacing search. Conversion rate held, and admissions contacted more Google Ads inquiries than in Q4 2025.
