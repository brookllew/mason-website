Case Study

Hampden Clothing

Date2023
IndustryFashion, Lifestyle
ServicesPaid Media
TeamECommerce Marketing

Summary

Hampden, a designer fashion boutique, achieved a 5.8X increase in revenue with Meta Shops.

Goal

Develop innovative campaign strategies and boost online sales.

Solution

Mason Interactive proposed leveraging out-of-the-box campaign strategies combining Meta Shops to drive incremental net revenue for Hampden while improving efficiency. Our approach focused on optimizing the Path to Purchase, utilizing Meta Shops with retargeting strategies to enhance customer engagement and drive conversions. By employing Meta Advantage+ catalog ads tailored for retargeting, we effectively reminded customers of previously browsed or purchased items across platforms like the website and Instagram shop. This streamlined the path to purchase, increasing conversion likelihood and driving revenue while reducing friction in the buying process. To inform targeting and optimization strategies, we utilized advanced audience insights and analyzed demographic, interest, and purchasing behavior data of Hampden’s target audience. This allowed for refined audience segmentation, ensuring the delivery of the right message to the right people at the right time. Continuous optimizations were made based on audience performance, enabling more effective ad delivery and increased campaign success. Rigorous testing and data-driven decision making played a crucial role in our approach. We closely monitored key metrics such as click-through rates, conversion rates, and return on ad spend for both Website and Shops campaigns. By collecting and analyzing data, we gained valuable insights into campaign performance, enabling informed decisions on budget allocation, targeting refinements, and creative adjustments. This iterative testing and optimization process ensured that campaigns were continually refined for maximum effectiveness and return on investment.
